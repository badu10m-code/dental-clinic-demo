Since 2013 · Andheri West

We built the clinic we wished existed when we were patients.

SmileCraft started with one operatory chair and a rule that still holds: no treatment plan is final until the patient has seen their own X-ray and heard the honest version.

How we work

Three habits we refuse to drop.

  • 01
    Nothing happens on day one without your say-so.

    First visits are for examination, X-rays and a conversation. Treatment begins on a later date, once you have had time to think.

  • 02
    Specialists for specialist work.

    An endodontist does root canals. An implantologist places implants. A paediatric-trained hand sees the children. Referrals happen inside one building.

  • 03
    The bill matches the estimate.

    Written estimates before we start, itemised bills after. If something unexpected shows up mid-treatment, we stop and talk before we continue.

The years

How the studio grew.

2013

Dr. Ananya opens a single-chair practice above a bakery on Veera Desai Road. Four patients in week one, two of them family.

2017

Dr. Rohan joins after his prosthodontics residency. The clinic moves two floors up and adds a dedicated sterilisation bay.

2021

Third chair, Class B autoclave, and a dedicated kids' corner with a ceiling screen. Children now look forward to checkups, which still surprises us.

2025

In-house CBCT scan for implant planning. Same-week appointments become the norm rather than the exception.
