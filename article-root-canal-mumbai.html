Root canal in Mumbai: cost, pain and what actually happens in that chair

Dr. Ananya Sharma20 Sep 20267 min read

Most people arrive at our clinic having already decided the worst. A root canal has spent decades as the punchline for everything unpleasant, and the internet has not helped. So before the procedure, here is the version we tell patients sitting in the chair.

What the tooth is going through

Deep inside every tooth is a soft core of nerve and blood vessels. When decay reaches that core, the tissue gets infected and the body cannot clean it out on its own. The pressure builds. That is the throbbing that wakes you at 2 am. A root canal cleans the infected core out, shapes the space, seals it, and lets you keep the tooth.

What it feels like

The cleaning itself is pressure and vibration more than pain. You are numb for all of it. Most single-sitting cases at our clinic run about 90 minutes, and the honest review patients give afterwards is usually about their jaw aching from staying open, not the tooth.

The tooth that needs a root canal usually hurts before treatment. The week after treatment should feel better than the week before it. If it does not, come back; that is what reviews are for.

What it costs in Mumbai

Market-typical ranges in 2026: a front tooth runs roughly ₹3,500 to ₹6,000, a premolar ₹4,500 to ₹8,000, and a molar with three or four canals ₹5,000 to ₹12,000. Specialist clinics and microscope-assisted work sit at the higher end. Our own range is ₹4,000 to ₹9,000 per tooth, and the crown you will need afterwards runs ₹6,000 to ₹14,000 depending on material.

When we say no to the root canal

  • The tooth is cracked below the gumline
  • The supporting bone is gone
  • A previous root canal has failed twice
  • The tooth would cost more to save than a clean replacement costs

A dentist who never mentions extraction is not being gentle with you. Sometimes the kindest plan is a clean extraction and an implant, and a good clinic tells you that on day one.

The one-week version

Day one: treatment, mild soreness, an ordinary painkiller. Day two or three: sensitivity on biting, fading. Day seven: you have forgotten which tooth it was. A crown follows within two to four weeks, and that crown is what protects the investment for the next fifteen years.
