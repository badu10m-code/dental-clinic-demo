Treatment & pricing

Nine things we do well, priced in the open.

Every range below is what our clinic typically charges. Your exact figure comes after examination, in writing, and the final bill matches it unless you approve a change.

Endodontics

Root canal treatment

Single-sitting rotary root canals under rubber-dam isolation, done by our endodontist. Most teeth finish in one 90-minute visit; a crown follows in a week.

₹4,000 – ₹9,000 per tooth
Crown ₹6,000 – ₹14,000 depending on material.

Implantology

Dental implants

CBCT-planned implants from leading systems, placed by our implantologist with more than 3,200 placed to date. Includes the crown and all reviews in the first year.

₹28,000 – ₹45,000 per tooth
Full-arch options quoted after CBCT.

Orthodontics

Braces & clear aligners

Metal, ceramic or invisible aligners, with a digital smile simulation before you commit. EMI plans available through Bajaj Finserv.

₹35,000 – ₹1,20,000 full case
Depends on appliance and case length.
