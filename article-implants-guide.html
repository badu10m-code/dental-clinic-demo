Dental implants: the honest guide before you spend ₹30,000

Dr. Rohan Mehta28 Sep 20269 min read

An implant is a titanium root placed into the jaw, left to fuse for three to four months, then topped with a crown. That is the whole idea. The difference between a good result and a decade of small regrets is in the planning, and almost none of it is visible from the waiting room.

What a fair price looks like

In Mumbai, market-typical pricing for a single implant with crown runs from ₹25,000 for economy systems to ₹55,000 or more for premium Swiss and German systems. We charge ₹28,000 to ₹45,000 including the crown and first-year reviews. If a clinic quotes you ₹9,000, the implant exists, but ask which brand, which country it was made in, and what happens when you need a repair in year five. The cheap implant usually becomes the expensive one.

Ask any implantologist two questions: which system is going into your jaw, and will a CBCT scan be taken before the plan. Vague answers to either are a reason to keep walking.

The steps, in order

  • CBCT scan and planning: a 3D image of your jaw tells us the bone height and where the nerve runs.
  • Placement: one visit, about an hour, under local anaesthesia.
  • Healing: three to four months. You wear a temporary tooth during this time.
  • Crown: fixed after healing, adjusted against the opposing teeth.
  • Reviews: at six months and a year, then with your regular checkups.

Who is not a candidate yet

Uncontrolled diabetes, active gum disease, and heavy smoking all need handling first, in that order. Implants placed into unhealthy foundations fail, and honest clinics sequence the work instead of rushing it. A bone graft is common rather than dramatic; it adds cost and a couple of months, and we quote it before starting.

Implant versus bridge, briefly

A bridge grinds down two healthy neighbouring teeth to carry one replacement. An implant leaves the neighbours alone and replaces the bone stimulus the missing root used to provide. When the neighbouring teeth are already damaged, a bridge makes sense. When they are healthy, we almost always argue for the implant.
