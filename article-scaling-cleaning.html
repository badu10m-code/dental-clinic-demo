Scaling and cleaning: the ₹1,500 visit that saves ₹15,000

Dr. Ananya Sharma18 Oct 20265 min read

Nearly every expensive thing we treat in this clinic started as a cheap thing that was ignored. Gum disease is the clearest case, and the ₹1,500 scaling visit is the interruption point.

What that soft film on your teeth is doing

Plaque hardens into tartar within a day or two, and after that no brush reaches it. Tartar is a ledge where bacteria sit against the gum all day. The gum responds by swelling and bleeding, which patients read as damage from the brush and respond to by brushing less. It gets worse from there.

What scaling actually is

An ultrasonic scaler vibrates tartar off the tooth with water irrigation. It is not grinding the tooth; it is removing the rock that should not be there. Deep stains from tea and cigarette smoke lift away with it. Sensitive teeth feel a little raw for a day or two, and the difference in how the mouth feels is immediate.

Bleeding gums are not normal. They are the first, cheapest, most reversible stage of gum disease. If your brush shows pink every morning, the appointment to book is a scaling, not a softer brush.

How often is genuine

Once a year for most people. Every six months for smokers, diabetics, and anyone with a history of gum pockets. Clinics that insist on quarterly scaling for everyone are selling; the interval should come from your gum condition, not from a subscription model.

And the myth worth killing

Scaling does not loosen teeth. Teeth that feel loose after a scaling were held in place by tartar propping them like bricks in a wall, which is as bad as it sounds. Removing the prop reveals a problem that already existed, and treating it early is exactly why we remove it.
