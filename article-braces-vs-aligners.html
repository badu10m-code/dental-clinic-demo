Braces or clear aligners? A choice that fits your life

Dr. Rohan Mehta5 Oct 20266 min read

The question is rarely which appliance straightens teeth better. Both do, when the case suits them and the patient follows through. The real question is which one fits the life you actually live.

Metal braces

The workhorse. Roughly ₹35,000 to ₹55,000 for a full case in Mumbai. They work on complex movements, they cannot be lost, and there is no discipline question because nothing comes out. The costs are visible ones: food restrictions, cleaning difficulties, and the look.

Ceramic braces

Same mechanics, tooth-coloured brackets, around ₹45,000 to ₹70,000. Slightly larger, slightly more fragile, and popular with college students who want the reliability of fixed braces without the full metal smile.

Clear aligners

Removable plastic trays changed every one to two weeks, ₹65,000 to ₹1,20,000 for most cases. Eat anything, brush normally, almost invisible in photos. The trade-off people underestimate is discipline: aligners work only when they are in your mouth for 20 to 22 hours a day. We have watched more aligner cases drift from missed hours than from difficult teeth.

Aligners suit adults with self-discipline and mild to moderate crowding. For complex bite corrections, or for a fourteen-year-old who will leave trays in a lunchbox, fixed braces remain the safer bet.

What nobody tells you about either

Teeth move because bone remodels around steady, gentle pressure. That takes months no appliance can skip, and it takes retainers forever afterwards. The patients unhappy with orthodontic treatment are almost never unhappy with the alignment; they are unhappy because they stopped wearing their retainer. Budget for the retainer the way you budget for the braces.

Dr. Rohan Mehta

Prosthodontist and implantologist at SmileCraft Dental Studio, Andheri West, Mumbai. Three thousand implants placed, and still happier talking patients out of unnecessary work.
